Running a session

  1. Open the host controls on your main monitor.
  2. Open the display on your second monitor and fullscreen it with F11.
  3. In Teams or Discord, share the display window specifically — not your whole screen.

The host window shows the answers and the controls. If you share your whole screen, everyone sees it.
